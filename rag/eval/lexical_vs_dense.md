## Wins

Lexical win: `POST /search` -> `fieldguide-runbook:0`. BM25's top hit is the runbook chunk that contains `POST /search` (`post` twice, `/search` once). Dense's top hit is `retrieval-notes:5`, which has neither token. It only has the separate word `search`.

Dense win: `What is a cross-encoder?` -> `retrieval-notes:2`. Dense's top hit is the chunk that says a cross-encoder scores a pair (`cross-encoder` five times, and no `what`). BM25's top hit is `retrieval-notes:5`. That chunk contains the token `what` once and `cross-encoder` once, and it is about stored passage vectors. The token `what` occurs in only three chunks in the corpus, so the question word outranks the definition.
